Alignment & mobility

Hatha Yoga

Classic posture and breath control

Slow-paced classes focused on correct body alignment, joint mobility, and holding postures (Asanas) with steady breathing (Pranayama). Ideal for beginners, seniors, and anyone recovering from desk-job stiffness.

Best for: Beginners and anyone who prefers an unhurried pace.

Strength & stamina

Power Yoga

Active sequences for strength and stamina

A dynamic, faster-paced practice that links breath with continuous movement. Designed to build core strength, improve cardiovascular endurance, and support healthy weight management.

Best for: People who enjoy a more active, flowing practice.

Breath & meditation

Guided Meditation & Pranayama

Breathing techniques for mental clarity

Guided breathing and meditation with clear instructions, a comfortable seated practice, and time for quiet reflection. The teacher offers options so you can work at a pace that suits you.

Best for: People who want a quieter session with time to pause.

Women’s health

Women’s Special Batches

Dedicated batches for women’s health

Comfortable, supportive group classes for women, with adaptable movement, breathing, and rest. Discuss your needs with the teacher so the practice can be adjusted to your experience and comfort.
