Your First Yoga Class: A Simple Guide

What to bring, what to ask, and how to make your first visit feel a little more familiar.

Start with a conversation

You do not need to know the names of the postures before you join a class. Tell the teacher whether you are new to yoga, returning after a break, or looking for a different pace. Ask which format might suit you, and confirm the class time, trial fee and studio directions before your visit.

Bring a few simple things

Choose comfortable clothing that lets you move freely, and bring a bottle of water. Ask the studio whether a mat is available or whether you should bring your own. Keep your first visit simple: you do not need a collection of special equipment to begin.

Give yourself time to settle in

Aim to arrive a few minutes before the class starts so you can meet the teacher and find a place for your mat. Let the teacher know if there is anything about the practice that you would like to discuss privately. It is fine to ask for a clearer explanation or a different option.

Make the next step your own

After the class, think about the pace, the instructions and how comfortable you felt asking questions. A useful first session helps you understand the practice and the kind of guidance you want. Talk with the teacher about a realistic way to fit classes into your week.
